# SKSUScholarSync Role Functions

## Student

- Request a Certificate of No Scholarship and upload an Official Receipt.
- Track request status and download an approved certificate.
- Browse published scholarship opportunities and use official external application links.

## Administrator

- Manage accounts, roles, campus assignments, agencies, programs, and opportunities.
- Verify Official Receipts and process certificate requests.
- Monitor university-wide records, audits, and reports.

## Scholarship Chairman

- Upload name-only agency masterlists and automatically distribute matched records by campus.
- Compare scholar names with Registrar data as enrolled, no COR printed, or unenrolled.
- Monitor campus validation progress and review submitted results.
- Approve, reject, and finalize scholar records.

## Campus Scholarship Coordinator

- Access scholar records for the assigned campus only.
- Initiate microservice validation, review results, and coordinate discrepancies.
- Submit completed campus validation results to the Chairman.

## Campus Registrar

- Maintain and verify enrollment records for the assigned campus only.
- Use validation results to resolve missing, unmatched, or inconsistent records.
- Provide verification results to the Campus Scholarship Coordinator.

Scholarship agencies are administrator-managed records, not login users. The validation microservice is a technical component used by authorized Coordinators and Registrars, not a user role.
